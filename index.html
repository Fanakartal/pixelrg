(PIXEL)² Lab: People and Players: Interaction and Intelligence eXperience, XR Embodiment, Emotion and Language Learning Lab

We are a human-computer interaction (HCI) lab focused on Player-Computer Interaction and Human-AI Interaction, drawing on machine learning, language models, reinforcement learning and XR.

Our researchJoin the lab

Research themes

Player-Computer Interaction

How people play: player experience, motivation and behavior in games and playful systems.

Human-AI Interaction Experience

The experience of interacting with intelligent systems, from trust to collaboration.

Machine Learning and LLMs

Language learning for machines: language models as interactive partners and tools. We mostly study the user experience of these systems rather than building LLMs.

Reinforcement Learning and Emotion

Emotion-driven learning: agents and adaptive systems that learn from affect and feedback.

XR Embodiment

Virtual and augmented reality, presence and embodied interaction.
